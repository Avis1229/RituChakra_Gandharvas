# Product Guidelines: Field Shift

## 1. Brand Identity & Design Language
- **Brand Name:** Field Shift (Project RituChakra)
- **Team Identity:** Team Gandharvas (NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026)
- **Visual Motif:** Clean, modern agricultural-aerospace fusion. Uses earth greens (`#276749`), deep satellite blues (`#2b6cb0`), and warm warning/accent tones (`#d69e2e`, `#e53e3e`).
- **Typography & Aesthetics:** High-contrast, clean sans-serif typography with card-based modular layouts and clear visual hierarchies for multi-criteria decision comparison.

## 2. Voice & Tone
- **Authoritative & Scientific:** Grounded in NASA Earth Observation datasets (NASA POWER, SMAP L3, ECOSTRESS) and validated FAO GAEZ / ICAR agricultural benchmarks.
- **Empathetic & Actionable:** Prioritizes practical advice for farmers—plain-language explanations over esoteric sensor metrics, focusing on actionable steps ("Shift planting by 12 days", "Save 60% irrigation water").
- **Transparent & Calibrated:** Clearly highlights data provenance (e.g., distinguishing raw 9km satellite predictions from ground-truth calibrated readings).

## 3. UX & Interaction Principles
- **Zero-Friction Discovery:** Immediate access to preset agricultural hubs (Vidarbha, Bundelkhand, Punjab, etc.) with pre-configured soil and crop profiles.
- **Cognitive Load Reduction:** Multi-criteria trade-offs (Suitability %, Water Need mm, Soil N balance, Risk) presented side-by-side in clear cards and interactive Plotly figures rather than dense tables alone.
- **Offline & Low-Bandwidth Resilience:** Visual loading feedback with non-blocking cached fallback data so the dashboard never fails even under degraded connectivity.
- **Exportable Utility:** One-click downloadable agronomic advisory summaries for physical distribution or sharing via messaging channels.
